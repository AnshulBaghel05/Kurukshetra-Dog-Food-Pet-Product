# Owner Photography & Image Asset Guide
### Kurukshetra Dog Food & Pet Products (HR Pets)

To maintain the authentic, warm, rural-premium feel of the website without relying on generic stock photos, use the following shot list when capturing new photos with your smartphone (portrait mode or DSLR):

---

## 1. Hero Reel & Banners (16:9 Aspect Ratio, Landscape)
* **Shot 1 (Signature Reel)**: German Shepherd and Labrador resting together on a traditional woven charpai cot in Haryana sunlight (terracotta pots, rustic brick wall in background).
* **Shot 2 (Open Kennel Run)**: Wide landscape of the shaded, green, open-air play runs in Kurukshetra with adult dogs in motion.
* **Shot 3 (Store Counter)**: Clean, well-lit shot of the retail shelves in Thanesar stocked with Royal Canin, Drools, and Farmina bags with fresh stainless steel bowls displayed.

---

## 2. Puppy Portraits (4:3 Aspect Ratio)
* **Lighting**: Soft natural morning window light (avoid harsh midday flash).
* **Background**: Warm natural tones (terracotta linen, plain unbleached canvas cloth, or clean green lawn).
* **Shots per Puppy**:
  1. Eye-level sitting front portrait showing clear eyes and alert ear posture.
  2. Side profile showcasing bone structure and coat length (especially for long-coat GSDs).
  3. Playful candid with a natural hemp rope or rubber chew toy.
  4. Mother and puppy together to showcase maternal care and maternal temperament.

---

## 3. Product Shots (4:3 or 1:1 Aspect Ratio)
* **Setup**: Place product bags on a clean wooden table or travertine stone surface with natural diffused side lighting.
* **Details**: Ensure brand text (e.g. "Royal Canin Maxi Puppy", "15 kg") is sharp and legible.
* **Add Context**: A clean stainless steel bowl or sample kibble next to the bag increases buyer trust.

---

## 4. Optimization Standards
* **Format**: WebP or AVIF (convert from JPG/PNG using tools like Squoosh.app).
* **Dimensions**: Max 1200px width for hero images; max 800px width for puppy cards; max 600px width for product cards.
* **File Size**: Target under 120KB per image for 4G speed in Thanesar and in-app Instagram browser browsing.
